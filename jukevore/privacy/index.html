Jukevore privacy policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Jukevore is an offline music player for Android, made by Tiny Wrench Labs. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect anything, and it can't send anything anywhere.

No data collected or shared

  • Jukevore does not have the internet permission. It can't connect to the internet, so it can't send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code.
  • We (the developer) never receive any information about you, your device or your music.

What the app reads and stores on your phone

  • Your audio files. With your permission ("Music and audio", or "Files and media" on older Android versions), Jukevore reads the audio files on your phone and their tags (title, artist, album, album art, lyrics, volume levelling tags) so it can list and play them. It only reads them, except when you choose to delete a song (below).
  • What it saves, only on your phone: your playlists, recently played songs, the saved queue and play positions in long files, your settings, custom themes and any album art you set. All of it stays in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Deleting songs. If you choose Delete from device, Android shows its own confirmation first, and the file is deleted only if you agree.

Things you choose to share

  • Theme files. If you share or save a theme, it goes only where you choose (through Android's share sheet or file picker). A theme file holds colours, fonts and ASCII art, no personal data.
  • Equalizers. If you install an equalizer app, Jukevore lets it attach to its audio so it can adjust the sound. No information about you is passed on.
  • Android Auto. If you use Android Auto, your library and the song playing are shown on your car's screen through Android Auto on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Android's own backup

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include Jukevore's settings, custom themes and startup look in your device backup (for example to your Google account, or when moving to a new phone). This is handled by Android under your Google account settings, not by Jukevore, and we can't see it. Playlists, the queue and play history are not included. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Children

Jukevore isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Jukevore removes everything it stored on your phone. You can also clear it in Settings → Apps → Jukevore → Storage → Clear storage. Your music files are not touched by either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Any change that would let data leave your phone would be stated clearly here first.
